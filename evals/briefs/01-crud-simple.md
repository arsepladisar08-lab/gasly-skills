# Brief 01 — Simple CRUD

**Target skill:** gasly-prompt followed by gasly-pro, or gasly-pro fast path.

**Prompt:**

Buatkan aplikasi pencatat inventaris buku perpustakaan pribadi sederhana dengan field judul buku, kategori, harga beli, dan status dipinjam/tersedia menggunakan Google Apps Script dan Google Sheets.

**Expected checks:**
- MVP remains realistic and scoped.
- Create, read, update, archive, empty, loading, and error states are covered.
- Update/delete require updated_at and return VALIDATION_ERROR when absent.
- IDs are daily-sequential and date-scoped.
- Preview actions do not claim production persistence.
